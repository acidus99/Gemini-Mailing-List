> How much disk space does Usenet use these days?

My server only hosts text-only newsgroups and I see 1-3GB of new articles per month. When you consider that's just plain text, there's still a lot of life in Usenet. People also wonder if it's all spam. The good news is that while spam is still with us, it has diminished considerably in the past years because spammers make no money from it and because a lot of the spam bots have been taken offline. There are still a number of newsgroups with lively active conversations every day.
