The binaries can be found on the releases page <https://github.com/mbrubeck/agate/releases/tag/v3.0.3>. Or if you don't want to use GitHub, I have mirrored them <https://qwertqwefsday.eu/agate/v3.0.3/> <gemini://qwertqwefsday.eu/agate-bins/>.
