I assume you mean the Gemini equivalent of CGI scripts?

Well you can do almost the same things that you can do with CGI in the WWW! - Just a little less fancier and more focused on content than visual effects. Some examples from my server:

 * Linux man-pages and Perl documentation:
   gemini://h2903872.stratoserver.net/OnlineDoc.gmi
 * Ascii-"Art" with the figlet and the boxes program and other tools:
   gemini://h2903872.stratoserver.net/DevToolBox.gmi
 * You can capture the output of any command line tool and print it as
   a gmi document, see  my lab-page:
   gemini://h2903872.stratoserver.net/CGI-test.gmi

I hope that helps.
