I'm struggling to understand how a gemini client would help the girl on the 2nd hand PSP. There is literally no usecase there for gemini to have helped.  We could donate money to her local library to have a public PC or something. And if you are talking about infrastructure issues with that, well, the 2nd hand PSP connected, so that problem was solved a different way.

Gemini doesn't/won't replace the WWW, nor will it make it more accessible to underpowered hardware. I guess this person could figure out a gopher client on some ancient hardware. Then what? Would the girl be able to apply for temporary housing using gopher? Gemini would be no different.

I get it, it's cool to be able to use old hardware to access modern things. I have a bunch of old computers in a rack in my basement, I understand the desire.
