Hello all,
this is a short announcement for Tina, an aggregator for the Italian Geminispace.
You can find Tina at the IOSA capsule that I run:

=> gemini://gemini.iosa.it/tina/

Tina is named after Tina Zuccoli, an Italian teacher who had a passion for space exploration and managed to give small items from Italy to several missions including Gemini 3 and Apollo 11. A longer story is on my gemlog, in Italian:
gemini://gemini.iosa.it/gemlog/2021-05-06-tina-zuccoli.gmi
Finally, Tina is created with comitium. Thanks nytpu!

I hope this will encourage more people to create Gemini content in their native language.

Ciao!
